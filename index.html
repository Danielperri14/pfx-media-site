Common questions

Who is the Content System for?

Melbourne businesses that know they should be posting video but don't have the time or the team to make it. Cafés, gyms, trades, clinics, retail, real estate and service businesses all fit.

Can you tailor a package to my business?

Yes. The Content System is our standard package, but we can build something around what you need, whether that's more or fewer videos, a longer shoot, longer brand videos, event coverage or ongoing monthly content. Tell us what you're after on the call and we'll put it together.

What if I don't want to be on camera?

You don't have to be. We can build your videos around your team, your products, your space and your customers, with on-screen text or a voiceover carrying the message.

Do I need to plan anything?

No. On the strategy call we learn about your business, then we write the hooks and the shot list. On the day, you just show up.

Where do you shoot?

At your business, anywhere in Melbourne. If you'd like a different location, we can talk about it on the call.

How long until I get my videos?

All your videos are delivered within 7 business days of the shoot, colour graded, captioned and ready to post.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so we'll give you a clear, fixed quote on your free strategy call. No surprises and no lock-in.

Can we do it every month?

Yes. Plenty of businesses book the Content System each month so they never run out of content. There's no contract, so you can also do it just once.
